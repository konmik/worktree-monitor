- Worktrees show a right-aligned `[^N]` indicator for commits that are unpushed or unmerged.
- The selected local `main` branch, or `master` when `main` is absent, shows commits absent from every locally known remote branch.
- Other worktrees that share history with the selected mainline show only commits absent from that mainline. Merged worktrees do not inherit the mainline's unpushed count.
- Worktrees without a related mainline show commits absent from every locally known remote branch.
- The indicator is hidden when the count is zero or its complete text cannot fit.
- The indicator reserves room before the branch name is shortened and preserves refresh markers and worktree controls.
